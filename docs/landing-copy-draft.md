## Three promises (cards)
1. **You never type data.** Drivers send receipts from their phone. Fuel card statements and ELD mileage
   reports import in one step. We do the rest.
2. **Every number has a receipt behind it.** Two independent AI reads, deterministic checks, and a human
   reviewer. Missing or unreadable values are flagged, never invented.
3. **Audit-ready for four years.** Originals are stored unchanged, with a fingerprint and a full history of
   who changed what. Records are kept for the IFTA retention period.

## How it works (steps)
1. **Send** — Upload, email, or text your fuel receipts, statements and ELD mileage.
2. **We check** — AI extracts every field twice; rules catch duplicates, bad math, missing details.
3. **You approve** — Review only what needs you. One tap per correction.
4. **Download** — A quarter report, transactions CSV, supporting-document package, and an IFTA worksheet
   ready for your preparer.
